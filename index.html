04 / Project 01

Sign-to-Text Glove

A wearable smart glove on an STM32F103 that translates hand gestures into real-time text for speech-impaired users. Five flex sensors feed a multi-channel ADC, and a custom algorithm maps resistance changes to characters shown on an I2C 16x2 LCD.

STM32F103C++Flex SensorsADCI2C LCD
05 / Project 02

Precision Tilt Measurement

Real-time orientation tracking with an STM32 and the MPU6050 over I2C. Accelerometer and gyroscope data drive pitch and roll, fused with a complementary filter that suppresses noise and gyro drift, shown live on an OLED for industrial leveling.

STM32MPU6050I2CComplementary FilterOLED
06 / Project 03

Anti-Sleeping Glasses

An STM32 and IR-based safety wearable that watches for drowsiness and triggers real-time buzzer alerts, applying firmware development to safety-critical monitoring.
